Why a door that costs sats
  • The fee lands in the same Tether WDK treasury that pays the network fees of every payout. Revenue in, fees out, one balance.
  • Paying proves you paid, not who you are. The pass is bound to the browser that paid, for 24 hours. A production version adds a signature from the paying wallet so a pass is bound to a key.
  • Everything you do behind the door still needs a human approval before any RGB money moves.
